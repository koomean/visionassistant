# Asset credits

## Moon

`assets/moon.webp` is adapted from the full-Moon still in **Moon Phase and
Libration, 2021**, NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS 4874).

- Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualization by Ernie Wright;
  produced by David Ladd; scientific consulting by Noah Petro.
- Project page: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4874/>
- Original image: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004800/a004874/phase_full.3492.tif>
- Changes: transparent margin cropped; resized from 3240 × 3240 to
  1400 × 1400 pixels; encoded as WebP. The original alpha channel is retained.
- The SVS states that its content is public domain unless otherwise noted:
  <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/#frequently-asked-questions>.
- NASA media guidelines: <https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/>.

The image is used as decorative lunar imagery, not as an indication of NASA
endorsement or a real-time Moon phase.

## Social preview

`assets/social.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 screenshot of the website’s English lunar hero,
composed from the credited Moon image, fonts, and original website design.
It is a share preview, not a screenshot of the iPhone app.

## LunaV screenshots

These images come from the project owner's current local LunaV app previews.
They have been resized to 720 × 1565 pixels and encoded as WebP. No app interface
or results have been fabricated or composited.

| Website asset | Original preview |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/app-camera.webp` | `App/Preview/Voice-Camera.png` |
| `assets/app-answer.webp` | `App/Preview/Voice-Inline-Answer.png` |
| `assets/app-settings.webp` | `App/Preview/Voice-Settings.png` |
| `assets/app-text-sources.webp` | `App/Preview/Text-Sources.png` |

The camera, answer, and settings previews were captured on **21 October 2026**;
the text-source preview was captured on **20 October 2026**. These show the
Simulator interface and OCR examples before the 23 October voice-flow update,
not the new hold–talk–release gesture or cancellation states.

The previews are from iOS Simulator. The answer and text-source previews show
OCR of a synthetic test fixture. They do not establish live iPhone camera,
microphone, visual-model, or LiDAR performance. The camera preview explicitly
shows the Simulator test-image state.
